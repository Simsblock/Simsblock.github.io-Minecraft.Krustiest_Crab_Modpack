[LeashedPlayer](https://modrinth.com/mod/leashedplayer) allows you to leash other players.

---

## Controls

Leash other players by right-clicking them with a lead or leash your self to a fencepost by right clicking it while u've got noone leashed.

<Aside type="tip">
  Leashing can lead to some pretty funny sling shot szenarios.
</Aside>
